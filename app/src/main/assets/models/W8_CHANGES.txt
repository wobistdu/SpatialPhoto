Experimental SHARP768 weight-only derivative, 2026-11-21.

The provenance, Apple research model license and notices in LICENSE_MODEL and
NOTICE.txt continue to apply. Quantization does not expand the granted rights.

Changes relative to the app's SHARP768 FP16 derivative:
ONNX Runtime MatMulNBits quantization, 8 bits, symmetric blocks of 32,
MatMul constant weights only, accuracy_level=1. Activations remain floating
point. No retraining or distillation. Reproduce with tools/quantize_weight_only.py.

The default 0.34-w8 research build packages this derivative.
